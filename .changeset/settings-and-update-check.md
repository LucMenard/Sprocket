Sprocket has a **Settings** window, opened from the gear in the header, as in Tracket: the theme, updates, where your data is kept and what leaves your computer, and links to the project. Under **Updates**, **Check for updates** looks for a new version now and offers to install it, alongside the check Sprocket makes when it starts, which can be turned off there. The gear shows a dot while a new version waits to be installed.
